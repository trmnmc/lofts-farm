How Lofts Farm works

Three speeds. Crops are slow and are your supply constraint. The mill is fast and can always eat more than you can grow. Fertiliser is the toll between them.

Crops grow while you're away. The mill does not. Come back and the field has finished without you; the mill sat idle the whole time. That asymmetry is the design.

Seeds are items with durability. Buy them from the merchant, hold one, click a plot to sow. Cheap seeds carry many plantings; a pitcher seed carries two.

Keep each plot's crop makes every plot remember what it last grew and replant that on a click — so a mixed farm stays mixed without going back to the rack each time. Turn it off and every plot sows whatever is in your hand, which is faster when you want one crop in bulk. A plot with no memory, or one whose seed you've run out of, falls back to the seed in hand.

The order board is the game. Raw orders want crops straight from the field; milled orders want finished goods and pay considerably more. Orders decide what's worth growing — without them you'd just plant whichever crop pays best per second, forever.

The mill will eat stock you need. It grinds raw goods two at a time, which can starve a raw order of the very crop it wants. Hit Stop to halt everything, pause a single line, or set a keep number — the mill never grinds a crop below that, so a line can run continuously and still leave an order's worth on the shelf.

Combined orders unlock at level 7. Several goods, one deadline, one payout at about 40% over market. They're the reason to stop selling everything the moment you harvest it — and since the mill grinds one recipe at a time, an order wanting two milled goods is a scheduling problem, not just a shopping list.

Nothing ever takes money off you. Miss an order and you simply don't get paid. Prices move but never fall below what a crop is worth growing.

Automation is bought, not given. A farmhand ($1,500) clears every ripe plot at once; a seed drill ($3,000) sows every empty one; a seed supplier ($2,500) buys the seed you need the moment you sow it, so you never break off planting to restock. Until you own them you do each job a plot at a time — fine on four plots, less so on twelve. Once owned, H and R do the bulk jobs from the keyboard.

A plot the supplier will buy for shows a dotted edge and the price, so a click never spends money without telling you first. Pause the supplier any time from the merchant.
